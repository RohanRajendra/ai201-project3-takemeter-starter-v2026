# Label definitions for baseline.py, one per line as `label: definition`.
# Copied from the README's Label Taxonomy so the zero-shot baseline is judged on
# exactly the definitions the labels were made with.

evidence: backs its point with something specific a reader could check without taking the writer's word for it - a link, a named source, a quote, a version or CVE number, a date, or a number about the world
experience: backs its point with the writer's own first-hand experience - something specific they built, used, ran, or saw happen
assertion: makes a claim, judgment, or suggestion but offers neither checkable support nor first-hand experience for it
